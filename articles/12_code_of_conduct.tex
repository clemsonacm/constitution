\section*{Article XII: Code of Conduct}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Article XII: Code of Conduct}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Harassment is unwelcome or hostile behavior, including speech that intimidates, creates discomfort, or interferes with participation in chapter activities. Harassment in any form, including but not limited to harassment based on age, race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other status protected by law, will not be tolerated.
    \item The following behaviors are prohibited:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Actions that violate applicable Clemson University safety rules or manuals.
        \item Intimidation, stalking, or unwelcome physical contact.
        \item Harassing photography or recording, and the use of abusive or degrading language.
        \item Sexual imagery or unwelcome sexual attention.
    \end{enumerate}
    \item Reporting and resolution:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Members accused of violations will receive written notice of the alleged violation and have an opportunity to respond.
        \item If the violation involves safety or behavior that could lead to suspension or expulsion, the chapter advisor, or their designee, will decide on the appropriate action.
        \item Members may appeal the advisor’s decision to a member of the Clemson ACM Executive Board.
    \end{enumerate}
    \item Individuals violating these standards may face sanctions or be excluded from further participation at the discretion of chapter officers and the faculty advisor.
\end{enumerate}
